## Featured projects


### LiveMarket — a living market for survival servers

![LiveMarket shop GUI, captured in game](https://rconpilot.github.io/livemarket-shop.png)

A Paper plugin whose shop prices move with real supply & demand, saturate against farming, and drift back to base over time. Chest GUI with price charts, per-item volatility, crash-safe persistence, price alerts, PlaceholderAPI support, and a trading pause for admins.

[Repository](https://github.com/rconpilot/LiveMarket) · [Release v0.5.0](https://github.com/rconpilot/LiveMarket/releases/tag/v0.5.0)


### PaperPilot + Studio — an AI copilot for Paper servers

![PaperPilot Studio overview](https://rconpilot.github.io/paperpilot-studio.gif)

An MCP server that lets AI agents run a Paper server safely — tiered tools, a command allowlist and a full audit log — plus Studio, its native Windows app with a copilot that drafts actions for approval before running them.

[Repository](https://github.com/rconpilot/PaperPilot) · [Studio 2.1.0 (Tauri)](https://github.com/rconpilot/PaperPilot/releases/tag/studio-tauri-v2.1.0)


### ModelFramework — custom models as interactive blocks

![ModelFramework demo](https://rconpilot.github.io/modelframework-demo.gif)

A framework built on BetterModel that turns custom models into interactive Minecraft blocks — animations, interactions and configurable requirements, all defined in YAML.

[Repository](https://github.com/rconpilot/ModelFramework)


### NexLink — your phone's internet, on your PC

![NexLink](https://rconpilot.github.io/nexlink-card.png)

Share your phone's internet — with its VPN, DNS and proxy — to your PC over USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. A tiny Rust engine on the PC, a small Android companion with one arm switch. Manual connect, no proxy, no auto-retry.

[Repository](https://github.com/rconpilot/NexLink) · [Release v0.3.1](https://github.com/rconpilot/NexLink/releases/tag/v0.3.1)
